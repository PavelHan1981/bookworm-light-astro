## **What is BQB Certification?**


BQB stands for Bluetooth Qualification Body.


**Only products that pass certification by the Bluetooth SIG (Special Interest Group) are recognized as genuine Bluetooth products. Certification ensures product compatibility and performance meet Bluetooth standards and grants legal rights to use the Bluetooth logo and technical specifications. Unauthorized use of Bluetooth branding without qualification can result in fines.**


_When is BQB certification required?_


Any product featuring Bluetooth functionality must obtain Bluetooth BQB certification; otherwise, the Bluetooth SIG will consider the product an infringement. BQB certification is one of the core qualification requirements for Bluetooth products and is managed directly by the SIG.


## **BQB Certification Fees**


BQB qualification costs consist of two main components: DID (Declaration ID) fees and testing fees.

- The DID fee varies depending on the membership tier:
    - For small businesses with annual revenue under $1,000,000 USD, there are two opportunities within an 18-month period to purchase a DID at a discounted rate of $2,500 USD/DID (must be used within those 18 months; standard pricing applies thereafter).
    - For SIG Adopter Members (free membership): $8,000 USD/DID, unlimited purchases.
    - For SIG Associate Members:
        - Companies with annual revenue under $100M USD: Annual membership fee of $7,500 USD/year; $4,000 USD/DID, unlimited purchases.
        - Companies with annual revenue over $100M USD: Annual membership fee of $35,000 USD/year; $4,000 USD/DID, unlimited purchases.
- Testing fees are determined by the specific accredited test facility or laboratory.

## **Scope of Bluetooth BQB Certification**

- Radio Frequency (RF) Conformance Testing;
- Protocol Conformance Testing;
- Profile Interoperability Testing;
- Declaration of Conformity (DoC);
- Technical Documentation Review.

## **Bluetooth BQB Qualification Procedure**


1. Member creates the project online;


2. Member obtains a Bluetooth Design ID;


3. Member selects product features (PICS - Protocol Implementation Conformance Statement);


4. Member generates the Test Plan;


5. Member completes product development;


6. Member tests the product;


7. Member completes the test report;


8. Member submits the test report to the SIG;


9. Member publishes the listing online (QDL and EPL);


10. Member marks the product with the Listing ID;


11. Member signs the DoC and SDoC;


12. Member pays the declaration/listing fee;


## **Searching Bluetooth BQB Listings**


Once a Bluetooth product passes BQB certification testing, the SIG assigns it a unique QDID (Qualified Design ID), which can be verified using the official Bluetooth listing search tool.


Search URL: [Launch Studio - Listing Search (bluetooth.com)](https://launchstudio.bluetooth.com/Listings/Search);
