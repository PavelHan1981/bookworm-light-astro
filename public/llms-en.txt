# PavelHan.tech (English Knowledge Guide)

> PavelHan.tech is a technical blog maintained by Technology Expert Pavel Han, dedicated to "understanding and explaining technical topics from low-level fundamental principles." Articles cover physics and signal processing for mmWave radar sensing, deep learning models (YOLO, RT-DETR, ViT, SAM3) and NPU deployment (RK3588, LiteRT/TFLite), Transformer architectures and KV Cache optimization, audio signal processing (FFT, PANNs), IoT protocols (BLE, NFC, Webhooks), and hardware thermal engineering.

## Core Topics & Featured English Articles

### 1. mmWave Radar & Sensing Technology
- [Principle and Application Summary of mmWave Sensing](https://pavelhan.tech/en/article/2026-06-29-the-priciple-and-application-of-mmwave-sensing): Electromagnetic wave propagation, FMCW chirp signals, and mathematical principles for distance, speed, and angle estimation.
- [Detailed Summary of FMCW mmWave Sensing Workflow & Calculation](https://pavelhan.tech/en/article/2026-07-01-the-detailed-summary-of-mmWave-sensing-workflow-and-calculation): Step-by-step breakdown of Chirp generation, mixing, IF signal FFT spectrum analysis, and range-velocity resolution.
- [How Angle Information is Measured and Calculated in mmWave Radar](https://pavelhan.tech/en/article/2026-07-02-How-to-measure-the-angle-information-mmsense-radar): Antenna array (MIMO), phase difference, and 2D-FFT/DBF angle estimation principles.
- [How Point Cloud Data is Built in mmWave Radar System](https://pavelhan.tech/en/article/2026-07-08-How-the-point-cloud-data-of-mmwave-radar-built): Pipeline from raw ADC sampling to 3D Range-Doppler-Azimuth point cloud generation.
